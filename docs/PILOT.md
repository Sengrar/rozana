# PILOT: bakery and dairy wholesaler

Fill this in after talking to him. Needed before M1.

## Customers
- How many regular customers in total?
- How many are retailers/shops, and how many are households?
- How many order every day, and how many only sometimes?

## Orders
- How do orders reach him today (phone call, WhatsApp, register, walk-in)?
- By what time must tomorrow's order be in?
- How often do customers change quantities, and how late?
- How many mid-day or urgent orders in a normal day?

## Products and prices
- How many products? Sold by piece, packet, kg, litre, crate?
- Does every retailer pay the same rate, or do some have special rates?
- How often do rates change?

## Delivery
- How many delivery boys and how many routes?
- Does anything come back (crates, unsold bread, empty cans)?
- What happens today when an item is short?

## Money
- What share of sales is on credit?
- How is credit recorded today, and how often is it settled?
- Cash, UPI or both? Who collects it?
- Roughly how much is outstanding right now?

## His words
- What wastes most of his time each day?
- What mistake costs him the most money?
- What would make him pay for this?
