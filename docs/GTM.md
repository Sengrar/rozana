# GO-TO-MARKET

Status: PROPOSED starting position. Update after every sales conversation.

## Positioning
For wholesalers who deliver every day and also run a shop.
One line: "Your customers order on their phone at night. In the morning you get
one packing list, your delivery boy gets his route, and all credit is tracked.
Fixed monthly fee, no commission."

## Brand
- Name: short, Hindi, easy to say on a phone call (open decision O1)
- Trust signals: founder's face and phone number on the site, GST invoice for
  every payment, "your data is yours, export any time", a local office address
- Proof: the pilot bakery's case study with real numbers
- Support promise: a human on WhatsApp, in Hindi

## Leads
1. Design partner: the bakery and dairy wholesaler, free for 2 to 3 months in
   return for feedback, a testimonial and introductions.
2. Built-in loop: "Powered by <brand>" on every storefront, statement and
   invoice. His retailers are shopkeepers too.
3. Walk-in demos in the local wholesale market, phone in hand, using the
   pilot's live store.
4. Trader association WhatsApp groups and supplier networks.
5. Referral: one free month per paying referral.

## Conversion
1. Demo on their own data: load 10 of their products while sitting with them.
2. We do the setup (paid add-on, or free for the first few).
3. 15-day trial starts at the first real order, not at sign-up.
4. Day 10: show them their own numbers (orders taken, credit tracked).
5. If they hesitate: coupon or annual discount, from super admin.
6. Push annual plans; fewer renewal conversations.

## Objections and answers
- "My customers won't use an app": it opens from a WhatsApp link, nothing to install.
- "I already use a billing app": that records sales; this brings in the orders.
- "Too costly": less than one missed delivery or one forgotten credit a month.
- "What if you shut down": export all your data any time.

## Later revenue
- Automation add-ons: payment reminders, low-stock alerts, daily summary to
  the owner, supplier reorder messages.
- Setup and catalogue photography as a service.
